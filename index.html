Personal Story & Mission

Medicine Taught Me to Care for People. Money Taught Me to Change My Future.

Earlier in my career, I carried multiple six-figure debt despite earning a strong physician income. Like many women in medicine, I was leading clinical teams and making high-stakes decisions at the hospital—yet my personal finances felt overly complex and fragmented.

I realized I didn't need more shifts, another degree, or a complicated financial strategy. I needed to lead my money the way I lead everything else in healthcare: with clarity, calm decisiveness, and one meaningful priority at a time.

"By simplifying my finances and focusing on one meaningful priority at a time, I paid off the last $100,000 in 7 months and went on to build a multiple 7-figure portfolio."

As a practicing physician, experienced physician executive, and Certified Money Coach, I created The Physician Wealth School™ framework so women physicians nationwide can eliminate debt with intention and build lasting wealth without sacrificing the lifestyle they have earned.

  • Make clean, confident financial decisions without second-guessing.
  • Create real freedom, optionality, and flexibility in medicine and life.
